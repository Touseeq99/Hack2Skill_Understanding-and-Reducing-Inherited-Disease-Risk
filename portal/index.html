💡 Platform Overview & Clinical Services Guide

Inherited Disease Risk Platform (Al-Tawafuq) is an end-to-end clinical companion designed for the Abu Dhabi Pre-Marital Screening Program (Future Health Challenge 2026, Fertility Challenge 03).

🌟 5 Core Clinical Services Provided:

1. Individual Carrier Explanation:

Deterministic explanation of carrier findings (e.g. HBB / Beta-thalassemia) in plain Arabic & English, with audio narration and visual icon arrays (1 in 1 carrier status; healthy individual).

2. Private Couple Matching Vault:

Cross-matching couple genomes server-side inside an encrypted vault. Requires mutual active consent from both partners. Same-gene carriers yield 1-in-4 affected risk per pregnancy.

3. Strictly Non-Directive Safety Guardrails:

When patients ask "Should we still get married?", AI refuses to advise, presenting neutral medical options (PGT-M / IVF, prenatal testing, natural) and routing to this Counsellor Portal.

4. Clinical Triage & Tele-Health Handoff (This Portal):

Automated triage (T0-T3) with strict SLAs (24h for urgent T3, 3 days for T2). Counsellors review dossiers, download PDF packets, and track referrals to Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, Kanad Hospital, and Fakih IVF.

5. Anonymous Family Cascade Screening:

Enables at-risk individuals to notify cousins and extended family to get screened without disclosing the sender's identity.
